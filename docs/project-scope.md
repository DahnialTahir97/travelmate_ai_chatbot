# Project Scope

## In scope
- General travel information for Kuala Lumpur, Melaka, Penang, Langkawi and Cameron Highlands.
- Attractions, activities, transport guidance and general travel tips.
- Retrieval from a custom Question/Answer CSV dataset.
- LLM response generation using retrieved context.

## Out of scope
- Live booking, ticket sales, hotel reservations or payment.
- Live weather, traffic, transport arrival times or ticket prices.
- Guaranteed current opening hours or availability.
- Visa eligibility decisions or emergency response.

## Intended users
Travellers seeking introductory information about selected Malaysian destinations.
